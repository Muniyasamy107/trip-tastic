A FEW THINGS, BEFORE YOU GO

Good questions.
Honest answers.

Something else on your mind?
Tell us when you design your journey.

Is this a group tour or a private journey?+

Our collection is designed as privately planned journeys for your own travel party. These are suggested routes, not fixed-date group departures. Your vehicle, stays and experiences are discussed and confirmed in your personal quote.

Can we change the itinerary?+

Absolutely. Every route is a starting point. Tell us your dates, pace, interests and practical needs in the journey planner. We’ll use those details to shape a suitable proposal.

Are the prices and dates confirmed online?+

No. The displayed budgets are illustrative planning ranges, not live rates. Sending an enquiry does not reserve a room, vehicle or experience. Availability, final pricing, inclusions and payment terms must be confirmed in writing before you book.

Where do your journeys begin?+

Our suggested routes begin in Coimbatore. Alternative pick-up points and transfers can be requested. Any extra travel time or cost will need to be reflected in your final itinerary and quote.

What happens after I send an enquiry?+

Your details are securely recorded on this website’s server and you receive an enquiry reference. Save your trip brief for your records. Automatic email delivery is not enabled in this build; a booking is only confirmed through a separate written agreement.

What about accessibility and special requirements?+

Share mobility, dietary and other practical preferences in your enquiry without sending sensitive medical documents. Suitability, access and any specific arrangements must be checked with the relevant providers before confirmation.
