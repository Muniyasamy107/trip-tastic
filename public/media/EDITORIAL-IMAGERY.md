# Editorial imagery — South India Atelier

## New visual refresh, 20 October 2026

Four distinct AI-generated editorial mood illustrations were created for the
website's design refresh. These are not third-party location photographs, a
Trip-Tastic photographic commission, verified destination views, or evidence of
included accommodation, transport or experiences.

| Authored master | Mood / use | Dimensions | Encoded bytes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `valparai-cinematic.webp` | Valparai-inspired misty tea hills, winding road and a small SUV; collection / manifesto / journal | 1264 × 848 | 176172 |
| `coorg-cinematic.webp` | Coorg-inspired veranda, coffee-country greenery and warm lights; collection / React Quiet chapter | 1264 × 848 | 198312 |
| `nilgiris-cinematic.webp` | Nilgiri-inspired mountain train and stone viaduct; collection / journal | 1264 × 848 | 247216 |
| `kodai-cinematic.webp` | Kodaikanal-inspired misty water, trees and a rowboat; collection | 1264 × 848 | 226162 |

The veranda is a fictional architectural mood, **not a confirmed property or
supplier**. The train/viaduct is a creative composition, **not an identified or
verified railway bridge**. Place names describe inspiration only; do not use these
images as documentary evidence of a route, landmark, vehicle or booking inclusion.

Source generated PNGs are scratch assets in ignored `.cache/`, not deployable
files. The checked-in WebPs have metadata stripped, with no claimed GPS data.
Master WebPs use quality 85; `-640.webp` and `-960.webp` variants use quality 83.
Width descriptors are actual encoded widths, not claims of higher resolution.
`editorial-media.js` supplies allowlisted `srcset` candidates for journey cards,
detail pages, journal and the React Quiet chapter; the static manifesto has its
own equivalent markup. Browser selection depends on layout, device pixel ratio,
cache and connection. All four masters are under 250 KB individually. No image
is described as 8K or upscaled to simulate detail.

The earlier `western-ghats-cinematic.jpg` and `kerala-editorial.jpg` remain
separate AI-created mood references for Munnar and the Kerala backwaters. There
are now six distinct image URLs across the six authored journey cards.

The collection includes a visible AI-image disclosure and an “About the imagery”
button. The existing Visuals & Transparency panel also identifies AI imagery,
fictional property/railway details, and the original logo/self-hosted fonts.
Before launch, the operator should review branding/likeness and advertising
accuracy, and replace these mood illustrations with verified supplier imagery
where location/property fidelity is important. Final actual arrangements belong
in the customer's written quote.
